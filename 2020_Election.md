# 2020 Election

## I Stand With ([link](https://www.isidewith.com/elections/2020-presidential-quiz))

### Domestic Policy

**Q:** Should there be more restrictions on the current process of purchasing a gun?
- No, only for criminals and the mentally ill

**Facts:** Fugitives, those convicted of a felony with a sentence exceeding 1 year, past or present, and those who were involuntarily admitted to a mental facility are prohibited from purchasing a firearm; unless rights restored. Forty-four states have a provision in their state constitutions similar to the Second Amendment to the United States Constitution, which protects the right to keep and bear arms. The exceptions are California, Iowa, Maryland, Minnesota, New Jersey, and New York.

**Q:** Should teachers be allowed to carry guns at school?
- Yes, schools should require at least one teacher or security guard to be armed
    - if the teacher is carrying, they would have been trained in the class that allowed them to get the carrying license. Otherwise, there should be a security guard/officer.

**Q:** Should victims of gun violence be allowed to sue firearms dealers and manufacturers?
- No.
    - the gun manufacturer has nothing to do with crimes commited with their firearm. If proper background checks and laws were followed in the sale, the dealer is not at fault for the actions a criminal took with their firearm. Sue the family of the perpetrator if the justice system fails you.
    
**Q:** Are you in favor of decriminalizing drug use?
- Yes, for most but not all drugs
    - I look at this strictly as the crime of using a drug. If there is no victim, is it really a crime? If someone chooses to use drugs, that is their choice. Should using drugs alone be considered a crime? I don't think so. If a crime is commited while someone is on drugs, well, then a CRIME was commited so appropriate action should be taken. Should selling & trafficing drugs be a crime? yes. Simply possessing a personal amount of a drug and using that drug with **NO HARM** to others **including your own children** should not be a crime.
    
**Q:** Do you support affirmative action programs? 
**what is this?** Affirmative action is a policy that encourages the increased representation of members of a minority group. In the U.S. these policies are often enacted by employers and educational institutions in education or employment. NOTE: key word for me is encourages, not require.
- Yes, however
    - this needs to be done in a way that solves the unerlying problem in question. Minorities should not be given top priority in a job market in which they do not bring the same or greater level of skill to the job than other applicants. If we want to solve the problem of prejudice in the job and education markets, there is more that needs to be done at a deeper level and these programms need to focus on the development of minorities from a young age and giving them what they need in order to have a chance to succeed and not grow up in a broken, unhealthy home and environment.

**Q:** Should it be illegal to burn the American flag?
- Yes
    - If you don't like the contry, leave or do everything in your power to make changes. There is no room for a culture in any place of our lives that involves destruction of what represents us. People fight for your freedom in this country day in and day out. Thousands and thousands have died protecting and wearing that flag. Free SPEECH does not involve burning a flag. Find another way to speak freely. Put up posters, run for a position of office, don't burn the flag that represents your freedom in this country.
    
**Q:** Do you support the Patriot Act?
**what is this?** In response to the 9/11 terrorist attacks, the Patriot Act expanded intelligence gathering capabilities including: monitoring of foreign financial transactions, detaining and deporting immigrants suspected of terrorism, wiretaps, business record searches, and surveillance of individuals suspected of terrorist activities.
- Yes.
    - this only protects our country. if you are worried about being watched...what are you doing wrong? Do the right thing for the right reason **all the time** and fear NO man.

**Q:** Should the Supreme Court be reformed to include more seats and term limits on judges?
- Yes, but only reform to impose term limits on judges
    - I **do not** believe it is ok to add seats to the supreme court at this time. Imposing term limits will allow for the desired "democratic" election of supreme court officials over the years. If court justices are voted for upon completion of a term, this should keep the court in a state that represents the people of The United States. In summary, I believe the supreme court should have a term limit, BUT the number of seats should remain at the current number of nine.
    
**Q:** Should people on the “no-fly list” be banned from purchasing guns and ammunition?
- Yes, but not until the no-fly list screening process is improved for accuracy and includes due process
    - currently, the No Fly List has a history of producing false positives. If it is going to be used as a means to ban people from execising their constitutional right then the means by which a person is placed on the list needs to be more regulated. If the process is reformed to accurately track terrorist threat, then I do beleive that someone on a terrorist watch list should be banned from owning a gun. A terrorist should not be afforded our constitutional rights. Period. Constitutional rights are reserved for members of this country, not those trying to sabotage it.

### Economic Issues

**Q:** Should the U.S. raise taxes on the rich?
- No.
    - However, the tax system does need to be looked at. Large corporations should pay their fair share. I also believe there should be limits on the amount of tax cut benefits that one person can take advantage of. The rich already pay more taxes than the poor, so I don't think that gap needs to get even larger. Even having a flat tax would have the rich paying more. The loopholes seem to be the problem here. 

**Q:** Should the government raise the federal minimum wage?
- No, this will only cause prices to increase in a never ending cycle
    - Furthermore, minimum wage jobs are not meant to be a place to stay forever. Use a job of such nature to build experience and foster a working habit. I made minimum wage when I started my first job, thats how it should be. Pay is based off of what is appropriate for the duties being conducted and the experience and value that a person brings. Raising minumum wage will do NOTHING for the people that are stuck in those jobs for whatever reason, be it mental or physical. If you want a job that pays more, go fight for one, don't expect the government to raise you wage while you sit around content at the bottom.
    
**Q:** Should welfare recipients be tested for drugs?
- Yes, test anyone receiving money from the government including employees and politicians
    - People on welfare should be tested. If they are drug users, their welfare should be adjusted and they should be required to attend and recieve treatment. Potentially withhold their welfare and decrease by the amount that the treatment costs for that treatment period? When the person proves to be sober, pay them that welfare that they had witheld less the treatment cost? might be a good incentive to get clean and start contributing to society. I'm not claiming I know how to solves the underlying problem here, but bottom line is yes they need to be tested. Figuring out a way to do this while also getting these people off welfare would obviously be ideal.
    
**Q:** Should there be fewer or more restrictions on current welfare benefits?
- More
    - The current benefits should be capped. The benefits should supliment a working income rather than replace one. 
    
**Q:** Do you support a universal basic income program?
- No, this will encourage people not to work and harm economic growth
    - Furthermore, work for the job you want. Work for the money you want to earn. If you have a dream and a desire to do something you can do it. Go earn what you want, don't ever expect to be handed anything. TERRIBLE Idea.
    
**Q:** Should employers be required to pay men and women the same salary for the same job?
**what does this mean?** In 2014 the U.S. Senate blocked the Paycheck Fairness Act which would make it illegal for employers to pay unequal wages to men and women who perform the same work. The goals of the act were to make wages more transparent, require employers to prove that wage discrepancies are tied to legitimate business qualifications and not gender and prohibiting companies from taking retaliatory action against employees who raise concerns about gender-based wage discrimination.
- Yes
    - On the basis that `same job` == `comparable work`. A woman should no doubt be compensated the SAME as a man conducting EQUAL work in a position with EQUAL responsibility. There is no reason a woman should be discriminated against in the workplace. Furthermore, read up on `Kennedy's Equal Pay act of 1963` if you want history regarding this matter.
