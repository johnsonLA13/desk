# Acorn Squash

**1. Preheat Oven**
- 400 degrees

**2. Prep Squash**
- Microwave the squach for one minute. This will soften the squash a bit and make it easier to cut. 
- Cut the squash in half
- Take the seeds out
- Score the inside of the squash 
- Add butter and brown sugar to the inside of the squash halves
- Add the squash halves into an oven safe baking dish
- Fill the baking dish with 0.5 to 1.0 inch of water before baking. This will prevent the squash from burning in the oven.

**Bake**
- bake the squash for 1.0 to 1.25 hours. 
- remove from oven and baste with the butter brown sugar muxture at the bottom of the squash.
